A new Board tab: the parts with their pins as labels, no headers and no
wires.

- The chip's pins no longer run down the screen edges, and no wires go to
  the parts. The parts sit in two columns in the order you added them, and
  a part too wide for one column spans both
- Each lead ends in a label with the chip pin it goes to (PA8), in the
  colour of the pin's level: high, low, PWM, analog, or the bus colour for
  I2C and SPI. Parts on one bus carry the same labels, so nothing crosses
- A tap on a label opens the pin: what uses it, its alternate functions
- A new Pins button lists all 64 pins by number, with what uses each,
  which are free and which are kept free
- The sensors' readings sit under their modules
- On a narrow screen "+ Part" shrinks to "+"
- Settings: the header order and "spread the pins" are gone with the
  headers
