The Chip tab: what the registers, pins and clocks are set to, which Forth
word set them, and why a fault happened.

- The Registers tab is now the Chip tab: sections along the top, and a run
  bar along the bottom with the chip's state, Pause and Step. Core keeps
  what the Registers tab showed: the CPU's registers, Forth's stacks and
  the clocks
- Registers: every peripheral from ST's SVD, SysTick, NVIC and SCB
  included, grouped by whether their clock is on. Fields off their reset
  value are decoded ("MODER5 01 output", "AFRL2 0111 AF7 USART2_TX") and
  changes show in amber; a search box finds peripherals, registers and
  fields. A tapped field says what it does and what is wired to its pin,
  and gives the Forth that sets it, with Copy as Forth and Write
- Log: every register write, newest first: when, from which Forth word,
  and each field it changed from what to what. A loop is one entry with a
  count, the chip's checks come in between in amber, and filters pick
  writes, checks or one peripheral. A tap opens the register
- Pins: the package with each pin coloured by its set-up, and the pins in
  use with mode, alternate function, pull, level and what is wired. A pin
  set up otherwise than its wiring needs is flagged and listed first
- Clocks: the clock tree, live, from the oscillators through the PLL to
  each peripheral, with the flash wait states HCLK needs and a warning
  when the PLL is set out of range. A tapped box opens its fields
- Faults stop the chip, as a debugger would, and Core explains them: what
  happened, in which word and called from where, the instruction, and why
  it likely happened (a wrong address on the data stack, a divide by 0,
  the FPU off). Resume runs Stellaris' handler once more, Reset starts
  over
- Emulator: as on the chip, a flash error flag left set by an earlier
  operation makes the controller refuse the next program or erase.
  Stellaris 3.0.2 never clears them: after a stray store into flash, what
  you compile to flash is lost until a reset
- Fixes: the console's accessibility events carry only its last 2000
  characters (with a long console, a screen reader got all of it at every
  new character)

Known issue: the bundled Mecrisp-Stellaris 3.0.2 hooks the peripheral
interrupts (irq-exti0, irq-tim2 …) at another family's vector numbers, so
on the G431 a hook is not called for its interrupt and Stellaris prints
"Unhandled Interrupt" instead, here as on a board. SysTick and the faults
work. A fix is prepared for upstream.
