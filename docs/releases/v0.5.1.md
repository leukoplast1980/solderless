Faster: 0.5.0 had made the app noticeably slower, and this release fixes
that.

- Printing to the console no longer slows the app down: its whole text was
  laid out twice for every frame. A loop printing as fast as the serial
  port allows now draws at full frame rate
- Nothing is redrawn while nothing changes. The title, the Chip tab's run
  bar, the Board's warnings and the Scope's readout were set again many
  times a second, and each time the window was laid out again
- Chip → Registers refreshes in a few milliseconds instead of about 50
- The emulated chip runs faster: reading and writing a peripheral no
  longer searches every kind of peripheral (a driver polling an I2C or SPI
  status register: 16 % less work), a pin nothing is wired to skips the
  board's electrical model (toggling it: 13 % less), and the register log
  reads a register once per repeated write instead of twice
- The register log and the scope's trace are read on a thread of their
  own; the screen no longer waits for the chip to finish what it is
  running
- For developers: a Perf probe workflow measures speed and UI work per tab

Known issue, as in 0.5.0: the bundled Mecrisp-Stellaris 3.0.2 hooks the
peripheral interrupts at another family's vector numbers, so on the G431
its irq-* hooks are not called for their interrupts. SysTick and the
faults work.
