First release: the emulated STM32G431RB and its console.

- Runs the unmodified Mecrisp-Stellaris 3.0.2 for the G431; words compiled
  to flash are kept
- Cortex-M4 integer core with the full exception model, SysTick, DWT,
  bit-banding
- Every G431 register at its reset value; RCC and PWR with the clock tree
  and oscillator start-up times, the flash controller, GPIO, USART1-3,
  UART4 and LPUART1 routed through the pins to the console
- Messages from the chip model where a real chip stays silent (clock off,
  wait states, boost mode, baud rate, flash misuse, bus errors)
- Console, editor with completion and syntax colouring, Registers view with
  Pause and Step, projects, firmware loading (.bin, .hex, .elf)

Not yet: the breadboard and its parts, timers, ADC, DAC, I2C, SPI, DMA,
the FPU and DSP instructions. See docs/design.md for the plan.
