The breadboard.

- Board tab: the chip's pins as two headers along the long edges, in
  LQFP64-to-DIP adapter order or by port (Settings); parts in between with
  wires in the colour of their level, glowing LEDs, the pot's voltage, the
  piezo's frequency, floating and short warnings on the wire
- Parts: button (with contact bounce), LED, RGB LED, potentiometer,
  speaker, piezo. The app picks the pins and resistors, shows why, offers
  the other pins, and finds the smallest re-wire when a pin is taken
- wiring.fs with a constant for every pin, rewritten when the board
  changes; wiring list and parts list to share; the chip's support
  circuit
- Parts can be pinned over the Console and Editor
- Sound from the piezo and the speaker (PWM, edges, DAC samples)
- Emulator: EXTI, timers TIM1-4, 6-8, 15-17 (PWM, one-pulse, input
  capture), ADC1/2 with their bring-up sequence, DAC1; new chip messages
  for floating inputs, shorts, LED current, MOE, ADC set-up mistakes

Not yet: I2C and SPI parts, encoder mode, FPU and DSP instructions
(milestone 3).
